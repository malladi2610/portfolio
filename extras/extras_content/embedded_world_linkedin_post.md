Last week I had a chance to attend embedded world 2026, where I could meet some really great people who are working in the forefront of the innovation in Edge AI and making it a reality.

Start off with the innovation happening in the hardware side, I visited the Qualcomm booth to see their new Dragonwing I9 chip and had a chance to intreat with engineers who are building all kinds of robotics, Industrial automation and next-gen HMI solutions. 
moving on with the other innovations happening wrt hardware, I visited the Innatera where I had a detailed discussion with the CEO, Sumeet Kumar about neuromorphic computing paradigm and their latest pulsar neuromorphic chip, we discussed about how commercially viable neuromophic chips are, the development of the software ecosystems around them to aid in the penetration of them in the market and the application which are being made possible by it, one application that caught my intrest was the smoke detectors being acquied with their always-ion sensing technology that made the utilisation and the maintainance of these devices a lot easier which wasn't possible before.

Next I moved on to check the software innovations that are making application to run effectivley on the exisiting hardware, with this I first visited Incredibuild, who are building the solution to autonoously distributing the workload across the organisationally available idle CPU and cache reuse which aids in the faster build times and compile time and interacted with Peleng rand and Tsuyoshi Miyake to understand in detail on how it works, then I moved on to meet companies who are working on the model optimisation space and found a couple of intresting companies, one was Nota AI, who with the Netspresso framework are able to optimise any model for any hardware and here also I got a chance to interact with Tae-Ho Kim and we had a long discussion on how the tools works and also discuss direction in which the edge AI industry is heading and the challeneges and limitations of the current systems even after having promising results. Moving on with another company in the same space EnerxAI, they do something that I have seen for the first time IRL, they achieve a quatisation of 1.58 bits of LLM and able to achieve Real time Text-speech coversions on an edge hardware, when I read about this level of quantisation from microsoft in their paper about the 1 bit quantised LLM model I never though I would see a commercial solution and I had the discussion with Minwoo san, where I could undestand how they were able to achieve this level of quantisation and different application being build this.

As someone who is bullish about the evolution of EdgeAI, it was a great event where a lot of intresting companies have comeup really useful and intelligent solutions utilising the hardware and software developments that happened over the recent year.
